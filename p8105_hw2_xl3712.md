longitude, routes 1 to 11, entry, vending, entrance type and ADA
compliant. I first imported the NYC Transit Data, and cleaned the
variable names. Then, I select the relevant columns and converted the
entry variable from “YES” or “NO” to TRUE or FALSE. The dataset has 1868
rows and 19 columns. These data are not tidy because the routes served
are stored across 11 columns instead of one variable.
