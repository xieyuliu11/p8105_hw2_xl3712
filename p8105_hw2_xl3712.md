rows and 19 columns. These data are not tidy because the routes served
are stored across 11 columns instead of one variable.

``` r
Professor_Trash_Wheel_df =
  Professor_Trash_Wheel_df|>
  mutate(trash_wheel = "Professor",
         year = as.character(year))

Gwynnda_df = 
  Gwynnda_df|>
  mutate(trash_wheel = "Gwynnda",
         year = as.character(year))

Trash_Wheel_df = 
  bind_rows(
    Mr_Trash_Wheel_df,
    Professor_Trash_Wheel_df,
    Gwynnda_df
            )|>
  relocate(trash_wheel)
```

The resulting dataset contains 1188 observations and includes
information on trash wheel type, dumpster number, collection date, and
the amount and type of trash collected. Key variables include
`trash_wheel`, `dumpster`, `date`, `weight_tons`, `volume_cubic_yards`,
and variables measuring different types, such as `plastic_bottles` and
`polystyrene`.
